Straight answers

The questions
we get asked.

Are you licensed?
Yes. California licence 642852, General Building, held by Michael Anthony Guidotti. You can check it yourself on the CSLB licence register before you call anyone, and you should.
Will you actually show up and finish?
That is the whole complaint about this trade, and it is fair. What we can point at: a 5.0 rating from 17 reviews on Google, written by customers using their own names, and a BuildZoom score of 97, which puts us in the top 18 percent of 336,931 licensed contractors in California.
Is my job too small?
No. Small jobs are most of what we do. A sticking door and a full remodel get the same crew, and plenty of long standing customers started with a job that took an hour.
Do you work on rentals and tenant spaces?
Often. Turnkey tenant improvements, strip mall work, and repairs carried out with a tenant still in the unit. Coordination with a city or an HOA is part of the job when the work needs it.
What does it cost?
Jobs run from a single repair up to about $100,000. Send a photo and a line about what you need, and you get a number back.
Which areas do you cover?
San Jose, Campbell, Los Gatos, Los Altos, Morgan Hill, Palo Alto, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, Saratoga, Portola Valley, Mountain View and Santa Clara.
